01 · About

Code. Create. Solve.

I'm a Computer Science student specialising in Artificial Intelligence, and I like building things that solve real problems.

I started with Computer Science & Engineering at BRAC University in Dhaka, building my foundation in programming, algorithms and object-oriented design. At Swinburne I'm taking that into AI, software architecture, cloud computing and web development.

Before code, I spent years editing and producing video — including leading a small production team. That taught me to plan, collaborate and ship something polished. Now I bring that same energy to software: this year I've shipped a CNN that reads handwritten numbers, a four-role healthcare booking platform, a prize-wheel app for an in-store campaign, four browser games built without a single asset file — a 3D Snake on custom shaders, a parkour platformer whose art is generated in code, a Bangla-language game about surviving Dhaka traffic, and a helicopter rescue with a swinging chain of ragdoll survivors — an interactive 3D physics lab covering an entire national syllabus in two languages, and a 3D prayer guide in three.
